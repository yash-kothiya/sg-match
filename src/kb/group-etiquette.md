## Being on time and prepared
Respect for everyone's time is the most important habit of a good study group. Arrive a few minutes early, or log in early for an online session, so that you can start on time. Do the agreed preparation beforehand, such as reading a chapter or attempting the problems, because a session where people are still reading is a wasted session. If you are going to be late or cannot come, tell the group as early as you can, ideally the day before, and share any notes or questions you want covered. Bring the materials you need and keep distractions, such as notifications, to a minimum. When people consistently come on time and prepared, the group can spend its time on the hard parts, which is where a group helps most.

## Respectful disagreement
Study groups work because people see problems differently, so disagreement is useful, but it has to be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Say what you think and why, for example "I got a different answer because I used this step", and ask the other person to explain their reasoning before deciding who is right. Listen fully before replying, and avoid interrupting. Do not mock questions: asking for help is the point of the group, and a question that seems simple often reveals a gap that others share. If two people cannot agree, check the textbook or a trusted source, or note the question and ask a teacher. Keep the tone friendly, and thank people who point out your mistakes.

## Handling a no-show or leaving a group
Occasional absences happen. If someone misses a session without notice, reach out kindly first, since there may be a good reason, and agree what the group expects about attendance. If a person keeps missing sessions, the group owner can have a direct conversation and, if needed, remove the member to free the place for someone else. On SG Match, owners can remove members from the group page. If you need to leave a group, tell the others before you go and finish any task you promised, then use the Leave group option. Leaving is always allowed for members, but an owner cannot simply leave; an owner has to delete the group. Leaving politely keeps the door open to join other groups later.
